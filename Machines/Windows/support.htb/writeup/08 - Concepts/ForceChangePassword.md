# ForceChangePassword

`ForceChangePassword` — AD object-control permission/relationship bo‘lib, tegishli access-control konfiguratsiyasi mavjud bo‘lsa target account passwordini majburan o‘zgartirish imkonini bildiradi.

Labda `HALL.NOA@VISUAL.LOCAL` bilan bog‘langan `ForceChangePassword` edge ko‘ringan va undan keyin password-change bosqichi bajarilgan.
